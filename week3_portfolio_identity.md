## 4. Image and Visual Curation

### Final visuals kept

- Real screenshots and visuals from my AI and machine learning projects.
- Project-specific demonstrations where available.
- The robot hero video as a supporting visual that establishes the AI/technology theme.

### Real proof over generated visuals

For project sections, real screenshots and demonstrations are preferred over AI-generated images because they provide evidence that the work actually exists and works.

### Rejected visual

I would reject overly decorative AI-generated images that look impressive but do not provide evidence about my actual work.

Reason: the portfolio should frame the projects, not compete with them. A visually impressive generated image is not useful if it distracts from the actual project, results, or technical proof.

---

## Final Design Decision

The portfolio uses a consistent dark visual identity with Inter and JetBrains Mono typography and a single indigo accent. Decorative visuals are kept limited, while real project screenshots and demonstrations are prioritized because the goal is for the design to support the work rather than upstage it.
